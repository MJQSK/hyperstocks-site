HyperStocks Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 · Platforms: macOS, iOS, iPadOS, watchOS

HyperStocks does not collect, track, sell, or share any personal data. No account, login, wallet connection, or API key is required to use the app.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your watchlist, portfolio entries, price alerts, chart drawings, and display preferences are stored exclusively on your own devices (via Apple's local UserDefaults, App Group container, and direct peer-to-peer WatchConnectivity between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch so your Home Screen, Lock Screen, Watch Face, and Desktop widgets stay in sync). This data never leaves your devices and is never transmitted to the developer.

2. Public Market Data Requests

To display live prices, historical charts, fundamentals, and filings, HyperStocks connects directly from your device to public third-party market data APIs (such as Hyperliquid public market endpoints, Nasdaq public quote and filing endpoints, and Yahoo Finance public chart endpoints). Those requests contain only stock ticker symbols and timeframe parameters, never your identity, portfolio holdings, or personal information.

3. Analytics, Tracking & Advertising

  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No advertising trackers or cross-app tracking
  • No crash telemetry or behavioral profiling

4. Informational Disclaimer

HyperStocks is provided for informational and educational purposes only. Live prices, valuation metrics, portfolio calculations, and trading calculators do not constitute financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Please review our full Disclaimer and Terms of Use.

5. Contact & Support

For support or privacy inquiries, please visit the HyperStocks Support Page.
