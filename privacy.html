HyperStocks Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 · Platforms: Web, macOS, Windows, Android, iOS, iPadOS & watchOS

HyperStocks does not collect, track, sell, or share any personal data. No account, login, wallet connection, or API key is required to use the website or any downloadable app.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your watchlist, portfolio entries, price alerts, chart drawings, and display preferences are stored exclusively on your own device or browser (via browser localStorage on the Web, a local preferences file on Windows and Android, and Apple's local UserDefaults, App Group container, and peer-to-peer WatchConnectivity on macOS, iOS, iPadOS, and watchOS). This data never leaves your devices and is never transmitted to the creator or any developer server.

2. Public Market Data Requests

To display live prices, historical charts, fundamentals, and filings, HyperStocks connects directly from your device or browser to Hyperliquid's public read-only API and, in desktop and mobile builds, publicly accessible historical market and SEC filing endpoints. Those requests contain only stock ticker symbols and timeframe parameters, never your identity, portfolio holdings, or personal information.

3. Analytics, Tracking & Advertising

  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No advertising trackers or cross-app tracking
  • No crash telemetry or behavioral profiling

4. Educational & Informational Disclaimer

HyperStocks is a free, not-for-profit educational and teaching resource only. Live prices, valuation metrics, portfolio calculations, and trading calculators do not constitute financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Please review our full Disclaimer and Terms of Use.
