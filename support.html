HyperStocks Support

Version 3.8.0 · macOS, iOS & iPadOS

HyperStocks streams live Hyperliquid HIP-3 equity perpetuals, multi-year interactive charts, real-time stock insights, portfolio benchmark analytics, trading calculators, and native widgets—with no login or wallet required.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need an account or wallet? No. All market data streams directly from public APIs and your watchlist, holdings, alerts, and chart drawings stay stored privately on your device.
  • How do I add Desktop / Home Screen widgets? On macOS, right-click your desktop → Edit Widgets → search HyperStocks. On iPhone/iPad, long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen → tap + → search HyperStocks.
  • How do I refresh live data? Press ⌘R (Mac/iPad keyboard) or tap the refresh icon in the top-right bar to reconnect live WebSockets and reload market snapshots.
  • Keyboard shortcuts (Mac & iPad): ⌘1 Chart · ⌘2 2×2 Grid · ⌘3 Insights · ⌘4 Portfolio · ⌘5 Tools · ⌘K Search · ⌘+/⌘-/⌘0 Zoom.
