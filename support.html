Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need an account or wallet? No. All market data streams directly from public read-only APIs and your watchlist, holdings, alerts, and chart drawings stay stored privately on your own device or browser.
  • Why does the Mac/Windows app show extra fundamentals compared to the website? The native desktop apps include direct historical backfill and Nasdaq fundamental lookups, while the static GitHub Pages website connects directly from your browser to Hyperliquid's public API.
  • How do I add Desktop, Home Screen, or Apple Watch widgets? On macOS, right-click your desktop → Edit Widgets → search HyperStocks. On iPhone/iPad, long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen → tap + → search HyperStocks. On Apple Watch, customize your Watch Face or scroll the Smart Stack → tap + → select HyperStocks.
  • How do I refresh live data? Press ⌘R (Mac/iPad keyboard) or click the refresh icon in the top-right bar to reconnect live WebSockets and reload market snapshots.
  • Keyboard shortcuts (Mac, Windows & Web): ⌘1/Ctrl+1 Chart · ⌘2 2×2 Grid · ⌘3 Insights · ⌘4 Portfolio · ⌘5 Tools · ⌘K Search · ⌘+/⌘-/⌘0 Zoom.
